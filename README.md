## 🚀 Featured Project

### CarryGo – Intercity Parcel Delivery Platform

**Carry. Deliver. Earn.**

CarryGo is an independently conceived entrepreneurial project focused on reimagining intercity parcel delivery.

The concept connects people who want to send packages with travellers heading towards the same destination, creating an alternative approach to traditional intercity logistics.

Rather than keeping this as just a business idea, I took the initiative to independently develop a dedicated website to present and explore the concept.

**Project Highlights:**
- Independently conceived business idea.
- Dedicated website developed through self-initiative.
- Technology-driven approach to intercity logistics.
- Focus on connecting parcel senders and travellers.
- Exploration of practical solutions to real-world problems.
